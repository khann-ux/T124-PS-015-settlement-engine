🧠 2. The 4 Big Viva Questions

Q1: "Why is MDR NOT refunded when a customer requests a refund?"

The Golden Answer:
"In payment systems, MDR covers three components: Interchange paid to the issuing bank, Scheme Fees paid to Visa/Mastercard/NPCI, and the Aggregator's margin.

When a customer initiates a refund, card networks never refund the interchange fee. Furthermore, the aggregator incurs gateway bandwidth and fraud screening costs to process the reversal. If the aggregator refunded the MDR, it would suffer a direct financial loss on every return. Therefore, per RBI norms and BFSI standards, the aggregator retains the original MDR."

Q2: "How does holdback / rolling reserve release work, and what if it falls on a holiday?"

The Golden Answer:
"Under BR-07: HIGH-risk merchants have 10% held for 90 days; MEDIUM-risk have 5% held for 45 days; LOW-risk have 0%.
The release target date is: Settlement Date + Hold Days + 1 Day.

If that date falls on a weekend or a declared bank holiday, my CalendarService.roll_to_business_day() rolls the release forward to the next active business day, aligning disbursements with operational RBI RTGS/NEFT settlement rails."

Q3: "What happens if a chargeback creates a negative balance?"

The Golden Answer:
"Under BR-08, if nettings exceed today's settlement, the engine settles ₹0.00 and carries the deficit forward.

Critically, a negative balance completely blocks reserve releases. Even if a 90-day reserve has matured, funds remain in escrow until the merchant offsets their debt through subsequent positive sales. This eliminates credit risk and prevents defaulting merchants from withdrawing reserve funds while in debt."

Q4: "How does dynamic risk grading work, and how does a merchant recover?"

The Golden Answer:
"Under BR-09, the engine continuously monitors trailing 30 days:
Chargeback Ratio = Count(CB in 30d) / Count(Captures in 30d)
If the ratio strictly exceeds 1.00%, the merchant's risk tier worsens by one level the next day (e.g., LOW ➔ MEDIUM ➔ HIGH).

To recover, the merchant must maintain 60 consecutive clean calendar days with zero chargebacks. After 60 clean days, their risk grade recovers by one tier."
